# New kaiju story brief (third pair): Sloba (12) and Minturtle (16)

All rules, file formats and test requirements are exactly as in `docs/NEWKAIJU-BRIEF.md` (read it fully, but IGNORE its
Avocadrake/Tacowl character sections and use the ones below). Differences: these two have stories through **Worlds 1-8**,

`k9.ts` / `k18.ts` (they already go through World 7).

## Sloba (12): bubble-tea sloth
Look: a sleepy smiling sloth whose fur is milky brown bubble tea dotted with black tapioca pearls, cream face mask, long
curved claws, often hanging from a big pink bubble-tea straw. Skills: Vine Swing, Power Nap (heals, wakes with a
shockwave), Sloth Time (slows everything), ultimate Mega Snooze. Voice: see VOICE.md (sooo slow, *yaaawn*, falls asleep
mid-scene, unbothered, accidentally wise).
Arc (suggested, make it yours): she slept through the Chef going missing; she only woke because her bubble tea went cold
(the Chef always made it fresh). Goal: get the Chef back for a fresh bubble tea, at her own pace ("no rush"). Running gag:
she arrives late or asleep and still somehow gets there first. World 5 ending: a fresh bubble tea and the best nap of her
life. Act 2: the Leftovers make everything STALE, tea included; the Garden is where the tea leaves grow (her home turf in
World 8).
Nemesis (`"foe"`): **Jitters**, a hyperactive espresso-bean hummingbird who can't stand how slow she is ("Hurry UP!
Hurry hurry HURRY!"), signature move Espresso Zip.
ENDING painting (`still-12-end`): napping blissfully in a hammock between two tea cups in a sunny garden, a fresh bubble
tea in her claws, friends tiptoeing past. Intro painting: asleep upside down on a straw, smiling, the kitchen clock
spinning wildly behind her, an empty tea cup below.

## Minturtle (16): mint choc chip ice-cream turtle
Look: a teal turtle with a big scoop of mint choc chip ice cream on a waffle-cone shell, cool half-lidded smile, four
stubby legs, no arms. Skills: Scoop Lob (freezes), Waffle Wall (blocks monsters and shots), Chip Storm, ultimate Sundae
Drop. Voice: see VOICE.md (ultra-chill, "Stay frosty.", never panics except about melting).
Arc (suggested): when the Chef vanished the freezer was left open and is slowly warming up; Minturtle (who lives in it)
must find the Chef to close it before everyone inside melts, slow and steady, tortoise style. World 5 ending: the freezer
is fixed and everyone is frozen and fine. Act 2: summer heat in the Garden; Old Fruitcake wants everything to melt.
Nemesis: **Hot Fudge Hare**, a cocky speedy brownie rabbit dripping hot fudge (tortoise and hare!), always racing ahead
and napping smugly, signature move Boom Hop.
ENDING painting (`still-16-end`): chilling in the cool shade of a giant garden leaf on a hot day, ice cream perfectly
frozen, sipping a cold drink, friends relaxing around. Intro painting: at the edge of a counter looking at a tiny drip
from its scoop, sweating, the freezer door ajar far behind.
