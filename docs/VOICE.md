# How Kaiju Kitchen Quest characters talk

Write it like a Saturday-morning cartoon script that voice actors will read out loud. Big, warm, silly, punny.
If a line wouldn't sound natural shouted by a cartoon character, rewrite it.

## Do
- **Talk, don't narrate.** Characters react, interrupt, argue, brag, panic and cheer. Most scenes are a quick back-and-forth.
- **Ham up the pun.** Deliver it with gusto, groan at it, or have someone else groan at it ("Get it? Bluebeary... BERRY?" / "Please stop.").
- **Interjections and sound effects:** "Whoa!", "Yikes!", "Hah!", "Ooh!", "Aw, nuts!", "Hey!", STOMP!, FWOOSH!, KA-BLAM!, *gulp*.
- **Catchphrases and running gags** (see the voice sheets) — repeat them with a twist, don't explain them.
- **Comic timing through dialogue:** the setup from one character, the punchline from another, or a character undercutting themselves mid-sentence ("I'm not scared! ...Is that thing looking at me?").
- **Contractions, slang, kid logic.** "Gonna", "lemme", "nope", "y'know", "ya".
- Villains monologue and cackle: "Mwahaha!", "Hehehe...", "You fool!".

## Don't (these make text sound machine-written)
- No em dashes (—) or semicolons. Use "...", "!" or a new line instead, and at most one "..." per scene.
- No staccato three-fragment rhythm ("Cold. Crisp. Ready."). Short is fine; robotic isn't.
- No "Not X. Y." / "It's not X, it's Y." constructions, no tidy lists of three.
- No dry narrator punchlines ("It did not." / "Nobody answered."). The narrator is a cartoon announcer, used rarely (max one line in a scene, and not in most scenes): "Meanwhile, deep in the Fridge...", "Uh-oh!".
- No characters explaining their feelings or the moral ("I realise now that home is..."). Show it with a joke or an action.
- No fancy words: indeed, truly, simply, quietly, perhaps, merely, whilst, journey, embrace, testament, resolve.
- Don't start several lines in a row with the character's name or "I".

## Voice sheets
| Who | Voice |
|---|---|
| **Gobzilla Jr.** (strawberry kaiju kid) | Excitable little kid. "RAWR!" (tiny rawr). Hero-worships his big brother, brags then squeaks. Wants to be "Gobzilla MEDIUM". |
| **Bluebeary** (blueberry-muffin papa bear) | Gruff grumbly papa. "Hmph." Mutters, threatens monsters in a growl, melts instantly around the little Crumblets and tries to hide it. Counts his Crumblets. |
| **Pizzaraptor** (pizza raptor courier) | Fast-talking delivery dino. "Beep beep! Coming through!", "Special delivery!" Obsessed with the clock, tips and five-star reviews. |
| **Mothball** (marshmallow moth) | Dreamy and sing-song. "Ooooh, shiny..." Gets distracted by every light, thinks it might be the moon. Gentle but brave. |
| **Squidgeon** (jelly octopus scientist) | Over-excited mad scientist. "EUREKA!", "Fascinating!", science words used slightly wrong, dictates silly lab notes ("Note to self:"). |
| **Crabbage** (cheese crab gardener) | Cranky old gardener, "get off my lawn" energy. "Hmph!", "Kids these days." Walks sideways and is proud of it. Secretly soft. |
| **Toastbat** (toast bat thief) | Smooth wisecracking cat-burglar. "Heh.", heist talk ("the job", "the score"), too cool to admit he cares. |
| **Dumplord** (dumpling king) | Pompous kid-king. "Hear ye, hear ye!", the royal "We", numbered decrees nobody follows, huffy when ignored. |
| **Norigon** (ancient sushi dragon) | Wise old sensei who is secretly a goofball. Grand proverbs that go wrong or turn out to be about rice. "Hrrm." |
| **Chocomunch** (chocolate golem) | Caveman speech, always "Me ...": "Me SMASH!", "Me no like hot." Sweet and simple, scared of melting but brave. |
| **Hotchog** (hot-dog hedgehog) | Shy, stammers ("I-I-I..."), turns into a sports announcer when excited about grilling. Stammer fades as he grows braver. |
| **Pengwhip** (cookies-and-cream penguin) | Hyper and bouncy. "Wheeee!", can't brake, ice puns ("ice to meet you", "cool!"), homesick for the freezer. |
| **Avocadrake** (avocado dragon) | A young dragon who thinks he is a fearsome ancient wyrm: "BEHOLD!", booming voice that cracks into a squeak. Terrified of turning brown ("Am I browning? Be honest!"). Guac puns ("Holy guacamole!", "guac and roll", "that's the pits"). |
| **Tacowl** (taco owl) | Night-owl party host and DJ: "WHOOO's ready to party?!", hoots mid-sentence, fiesta energy, "Taco 'bout it!". Obsessed with Taco Tuesday. Wise-owl lines that turn out to be about snacks. |
| **Bubblegumbo** (bubblegum elephant) | Big, sweet, slow-talking gentle giant. "Elephants never forget!" (he forgets everything). Pops bubbles mid-sentence (*POP*), stretchy-gum puns ("let's stick together", "chew on that"). |
| **Velvaroo** (red velvet kangaroo boxer) | Brash ring announcer of her own life: "In THIS corner!", "Ding ding!", hops when excited, boxing and baking puns ("knead to win", "frosting on top", "hook, line and sinker... I mean, HOOK!"). Big heart. |
| **Narrator** | Cartoon announcer, rare: "Meanwhile...", "Uh-oh!", "Will our hero...?" |
| **The Mold Crown** (`mold`) | Creepy, giggly, oozy villain. "Hehehe...", "Come closer, little snack...". Short. |
| **Nemeses** (`foe`) | Classic cartoon rivals: boastful, sneery, catchphrases of their own, cackle, storm off when beaten ("This isn't over!"). |
| **World bosses** (`boss`) | BIG SHOUTY CAPS when corrupted; dazed, apologetic and a bit goofy once cured. |
