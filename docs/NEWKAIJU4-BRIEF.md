# New kaiju story brief (last pair): Bloblin (3) and Salmonster (14)

All rules, file formats and test requirements are exactly as in `docs/NEWKAIJU-BRIEF.md` (read it fully, but IGNORE its
Avocadrake/Tacowl character sections and use the ones below). Differences: these two have stories through **Worlds 1-9**,
so also read `docs/WORLD7-BRIEF.md`, `docs/WORLD8-BRIEF.md` and `docs/WORLD9-BRIEF.md` and write `w7-*`, `w8-*` and `w9-*`
(open / boss / after / elite and s2..s9) like the existing kaiju. Good models: `src/story/arcs/k8.ts` + `more/k8.ts`, and
the newest full stories `k12.ts` / `k16.ts` (they already go through World 8).

## Bloblin (3): lime jelly blob
Look: a glossy bright green lime jelly with a happy face, whipped cream and a cherry on top, a lime wedge in its side, two
tiny stubby nubs, no legs (it wobbles and hops). Skills: Jiggle Splat (glues), Jelly Clones, Wobble Shield, ultimate Jelly
Quake. Voice: see VOICE.md (bubbly wobbly cheerleader, "Wibble wobble!", jiggles when nervous, desperate to be invited to
the dessert table).
Arc (suggested, make it yours): Bloblin has never been picked for the Chef's dessert table ("too wobbly"); the Chef
promised a spot at the big dessert party, then vanished, so Bloblin sets out to bring the Chef back and finally get a
seat. Running gag: everyone forgets to invite Bloblin, and Bloblin cheers them on anyway. World 5 ending: the centre of the
dessert table, surrounded by friends. Act 2: the Leftovers' Feast of the Forgotten is the opposite of a party, and
forgotten foods feel what Bloblin used to feel; Bloblin might even feel a little sorry for them (good seed for World 10).
Nemesis (`"foe"`): **Grey Gelato**, a smug melting grey gelato in a snooty cone with a tiny moustache who thinks jelly is
"so last century" and always gets picked first; signature move Snooty Drip.
ENDING painting (`still-3-end`): wobbling happily at the centre of a big dessert table at a party, friends and desserts all
around, a big spoon raised in a toast. Intro painting: alone on a party plate next to a blank little flag, looking at a row
of empty dessert bowls, a little lonely but smiling.

## Salmonster (14): salmon fish monster
Look: a navy salmon standing up on its tail with a pink-orange salmon-steak belly, two side fins, a big toothy friendly
grin, a lemon slice on its head like a hat. No arms or legs. Skills: Upstream Leap, Tidal Push, Salmon Swarm, ultimate
Salmon Run. Voice: see VOICE.md (gung-ho sporty jock, "Let's GOOO!", fish puns, swims against the current on purpose).
Arc (suggested): Salmonster was on the Chef's shopping list for a fancy fish dinner, escaped the fish counter and wants to
swim "home" upstream to the river it remembers; the drain in the Sink is the first step home, so the Chef and the drain
lead the way. Running gag: it insists on going the HARD way (upstream, uphill, backwards) every single time. World 5
ending: it leaps up a sparkling river at sunrise. Act 2: it comes back for its friends because the Leftovers threaten the
fish counter and the Supermarket is its old turf (World 9 is its home).
Nemesis: **Grizzly Grizzle**, a big hungry burger-bun grizzly bear with a fishing rod who keeps trying to catch it for
dinner ("Here, fishy fishy!"); signature move Double Swipe.
ENDING painting (`still-14-end`): leaping joyfully upstream in a sparkling river at sunrise, other fish cheering on the
banks. Intro painting: leaping out of a sink full of water on a counter, looking up at a blank shopping list on the fridge
with a fish drawn on it.
